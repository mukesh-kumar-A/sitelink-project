Inspected and extended from the supplied **Project-Ready.zip**. This records what the existing prototype already provided, what was connected or added for the execution-intelligence loop, and what remains limited. It is not a claim of production validation.

Legend: **A** reuse existing, **B** extend existing, **C** new implementation, **D** optional/deferred.
